Engineer: What about the shared inbox?
Client: Our inbox gets around 80 emails a day from clients, lenders and inspectors, and someone has to sort them and forward them to the right agent. It takes about 2 hours a day.
Engineer: Any document work?
Client: Contracts and disclosure PDFs come in all the time. The coordinator reads each one to pull out the dates, prices and names, and types them into our CRM. We process about 25 a week at 20 minutes each.
Engineer: What happens outside office hours?
Client: Buyers call after hours and the voicemail fills up. We call back the next day, which is often too late.
Engineer: And property viewings?
Client: Showings are scheduled over lots of back and forth messages. It's about 30 minutes a day of coordination.
