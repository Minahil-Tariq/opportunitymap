Engineer: Thanks for your time. What does the business look like?
Client: We run an online clothing store doing around 300 orders a week with a team of 5.
Engineer: Where is the time going?
Client: Our support inbox is full of the same questions: where is my order, how do I return this, what size should I pick. Two people spend most of the day on it, roughly 6 hours a day in total.
Engineer: What about suppliers?
Client: Supplier invoices arrive as PDFs and our bookkeeper types every one into the accounting spreadsheet. About 40 invoices a week, around 6 minutes each.
Engineer: And your sales channels?
Client: Orders from Instagram and WhatsApp messages are copied by hand into our order system. It takes about 1 hour a day.
Engineer: Marketing?
Client: Our marketing is the same newsletter for everyone. We would love product recommendations based on what people browse and buy, but nobody has time to set that up.
Engineer: Anything weekly?
Client: Every Monday I pull sales numbers from three places into a spreadsheet for a weekly report. It takes about 3 hours a week.
